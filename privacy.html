Aither Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aither and Aither Dashboard handle information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you create or use an Aither Account, we may process information such as your name, email address, account identifier, email-verification status, and authentication/session information.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides authentication information needed to identify your account. Aither does not request or store your Google password.

2. How We Use Information

We use account information to create and maintain your Aither Account, authenticate you, keep you signed in, provide account features, and operate and secure Aither services.

3. Google Sign-In

Aither may provide Google Sign-In. Google authentication is handled using Google's authentication services. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's privacy practices and terms.

4. Local Device Storage

Aither Dashboard may store limited account and session information in your browser's local storage so the application can remember your account on that device. Passwords are not stored in local storage.

5. Service Providers

Aither may use third-party infrastructure and services to operate authentication, hosting, email, and other application functions. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your Aither Account and associated personal information, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain.

8. Children's Privacy

Aither is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, account-data requests, or deletion requests, please contact the Aither project through its official GitHub repository.
